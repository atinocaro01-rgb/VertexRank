Privacy policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026

Overview

This policy explains what information the VertexRank website and web app handle, and the choices you have. We aim to collect as little as we need.

This website

The VertexRank marketing website does not set cookies and does not use advertising or analytics trackers. Like almost every website, our hosting provider processes technical data such as your IP address and browser type in server logs to deliver the site and keep it secure.

The site loads the Open Sans typeface from Google Fonts, which means your browser makes a request to Google's servers when a page loads. Google receives your IP address as part of that request.

The VertexRank app

  • Your workspace, including the websites you add, scores, keywords and notes, is saved in your browser on your device. You can remove it at any time with the Clear all data option in the app sidebar, or by clearing your browser's site data.
  • To run a scan, the website address you enter is processed by VertexRank's servers so the page can be retrieved and analyzed.
  • Only scan websites you own or have permission to analyze.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. If we add third-party services that process personal data, we will describe them on this page.

Your choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information we hold about you. To make a request or ask a question, open an issue on GitHub.

Children

VertexRank is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the product changes. The date at the top of the page shows when it last changed.
